# Refund and cancellation policy

Applies to all ShopCo web orders.

## Cancellation before shipping

Orders in status `Pending`, `Paid` or `PaymentFailed` can be cancelled by support at the customer's request. Paid orders are refunded in full to the original payment method. Release any stock reservations held for the order.

## Refund window

Customers may return items within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Shipping fees are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or was the wrong item.

## Partial refunds

Partial refunds (for example one item of a multi-item order) are allowed. Refund the item price multiplied by the quantity returned.

## Approval limits

- Up to $500 per order: any support agent can approve.
- Over $500 per order: a support team lead must approve before the refund is issued.
- Over $2,000 per order: the operations manager must approve.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued within 2 business days of receiving the returned item. Card refunds take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on the customer's bank.

## Not refundable

Opened software licences and gift cards are not refundable.

## Failed payments

An order in `PaymentFailed` was never charged, so there is nothing to refund. Cancel the order if the customer no longer wants it.
